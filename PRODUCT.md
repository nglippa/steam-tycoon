# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Players of first-person incremental/management games.

## Product Purpose

City Tycoon — *Terra, in the world of Locke* — is a locally playable first-person incremental city game. Players restore and grow a steampunk industrial district from The Lowworks to Grand Terra.

## Positioning

A walkable, first-person incremental: properties are upgraded at physical on-site brass ledgers in a fully generated 3D city, not from an abstract spreadsheet menu.

## Operating Context

- Controls: WASD walk, mouse look (pointer lock, with automatic click-and-drag fallback), Shift sprint, Space jump, E interact, Tab city ledger, Esc close/pause, F3 debug overlay.
- Six physical properties with five named commissions each, foremen, civic works, district charters, research, discoveries, and prosperity eras (Lowworks → Recovery → Industry → Commerce → Innovation → Grand Terra).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- All architectural geometry, textures, citizens and sound are generated in the project; no asset CDN, account, cloud API, or paid service may be required.
- Runs in modern WebGL2 browsers (Chrome recommended).
- **Open decision:** distribution target (Steam desktop, browser, or both) is undecided.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and setting: "City Tycoon — Terra, in the world of Locke"; brass, copper, steam, and aether-machinery steampunk identity.

## Product Principles

- Upgrades should be visible in the world, not only in numbers.
- Everything ships self-contained and playable offline.
- Menus support the walkable city; they don't replace it.
