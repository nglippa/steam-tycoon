# Terra: anime-inspired steampunk

The direction is crisp, illustrated steampunk: readable character silhouettes, controlled shading, oxidized blue roofs, red brick, brass accents and warm windows. Keep the struggling Lowworks damp and smoky while letting repaired areas gain color and light.

## Implemented in this pass

- Original canvas-painted anime-inspired eyes, angular fringes, goggles, scarves, coat fasteners and boot details.
- Three-band toon lighting on citizens, restrained ink contours, and shared vertex-color materials to limit draw calls.
- Hip, knee and shoulder articulation, head turns, and independent walking phases.
- Pinned cloth geometry that ripples progressively toward its free edge, plus rotating spoked cart wheels.
- Higher-contrast procedural masonry and a cooler sky palette. Full atmosphere supports up to 2× device pixel density; performance quality remains at 1×.
- Restored windows align with their original openings; first repairs cover boarded shop windows.

## Next art milestone

This remains a procedural stylized city, not the final anime art target. The next substantial art pass should deepen silhouette variety with original character outfits and expressive work animations, distinctive business rooflines and machinery, painted architectural accents, and more deliberate cel shading on buildings. Judge these at street level while moving, in rain, and at night. Avoid blanket blur, bloom or outlines that obscure ledgers and street routes. Keep all assets local and original.
