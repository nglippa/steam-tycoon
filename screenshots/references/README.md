## Wanted (not available locally)

**Borderlands:** daylight, non-neon rendering with visible ink and painted surfaces. Prefer official press screenshots at 1920×1080 or better:
- Borderlands 2, Sanctuary: street level in daylight, with painted walls, signage and ink.
- Borderlands 2, Opportunity: a clean, prosperous settlement. The closest analogue to Grand Terra.
- Borderlands 2 or 3, a Pandora settlement in daylight (Three Horns, Lynchwood, the Droughts, Ascension Bluff): weathered industrial materials and material separation.
- A Borderlands 3 close shot of painted props or a character, showing hand-drawn edge lines and painted material breakup.

**Anime characters.** There are none on the board yet. We want sharp modern fantasy anime: elongated bodies of about 1:6 to 1:6.5 heads, long legs, open eyes with a clear iris and catchlight, and graphic hair made of large locks. Official key art or turnarounds only. No Ghibli or storybook style, no chibi, no western comic faces.
